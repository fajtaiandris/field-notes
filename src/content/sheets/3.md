The community garden was full of activity this afternoon. Neighbors were watering seedlings, clearing the paths, and sharing advice about which plants would do well in the cooler weather.


  I helped carry a few bags of soil and learned that the empty plot near the gate will become a patch of herbs. It was nice to work alongside people without needing to make much conversation.


  On the way home, I thought about how small shared efforts can change a place. A few hours of work made the garden feel more welcoming to everyone who passes by.
