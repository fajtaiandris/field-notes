We went to the farmers market early and found fresh pears, carrots, and a loaf of bread still warm from the oven. The vendors were cheerful, even as the crowd grew around the busiest stalls.


  After putting everything away at home, we made soup and shared lunch at the kitchen table. The recipe was simple, but the fresh vegetables made it taste like something special.


  The rest of the afternoon passed slowly. I read by the window while the rain came and went, grateful for a day with nowhere urgent to be.
