The rain stopped just before noon, leaving the sidewalks bright and the trees dripping onto the path. I took the longer route to the café and noticed a small bookshop that I had somehow missed before.


  Inside, the shelves were crowded with travel guides and old cookbooks. The owner recommended a collection of essays about local gardens, and I left with it tucked under my arm.


  Work went more smoothly in the afternoon. I wrote down the main questions, set aside the distracting details, and found a simple next step that had been hiding in plain sight.


  On my way home, I stopped at the market for apples and bread. The shop was busy, but everyone seemed content to wait and exchange a few friendly words.


  When the clouds finally cleared, sunlight came through the kitchen window and warmed the table. I made tea, read a few pages, and let the evening unfold without a plan.


  It was an ordinary day, but ordinary days are worth noticing. A good conversation, a useful idea, and a little fresh air were enough to make it feel complete.
