The library was busier than usual, with students filling the tables near the windows. I found a quiet seat upstairs and spent an hour reading about the history of the old railway line.


  On my way out, I noticed a display of local photographs. One showed the main street before the new shops were built, and another captured the same corner on a snowy winter morning.


  I walked home thinking about how familiar places keep changing around us. It is easy to overlook those changes until someone takes the time to record them.
